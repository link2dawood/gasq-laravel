# Phase 2: Vendor dashboard and estimate management

Deploy with `php artisan migrate --force` after Phase 1 (both migrations may be deployed together). No new environment variables are required.

## Vendor workflow

1. Open **Estimates** in the vendor dashboard navigation. It shows new enquiries, qualified leads, draft estimates, and ready estimates.
2. Open **Buyer enquiries**, review the contact information and submitted service requirements, then choose **Prepare estimate**.
3. Review the prefilled scope and weekly hours. Enter the hourly bill rate, total weekly billable service hours, service weeks per year (1–52), one-time cost, validity date, and terms. Rate and hours must be supplied by the vendor; the application does not infer a quote from the buyer's free-text request.
4. Save as **Draft**, **Ready**, or **Archived**. A lead can have multiple estimates. Its **View estimates** link lists all estimates for that lead. The Estimates screen also filters by status.
5. On a Ready estimate with a current validity date, enter an 8–32 character PDF password and download. The password is required to open the PDF and is never saved or included in the PDF. Share it with the recipient separately.

Estimate numbers are unique and generated by the server. Buyer contact details are captured when an estimate is created. The currency is taken from the vendor's supported account currency (USD fallback) and retained on that estimate. Entered prices are already in that currency; they are not converted from USD. Currency, attribution, buyer snapshot, and estimate number cannot be changed through posted form fields.

## Pricing

Weekly recurring cost is hourly rate × total weekly service hours, rounded to cents. Annual recurring cost is weekly cost × service weeks. Average monthly recurring cost is annual recurring cost ÷ 12. The first-year total adds the one-time cost once. All totals are recalculated from saved inputs; posted totals are ignored. The PDF prints these assumptions and the saved scope and terms.

Draft, archived, and expired estimates cannot be downloaded. Expired estimates may be retained or archived; to make one Ready again, update its validity date. Editing and saving an estimate changes its next PDF download; it does not alter previously downloaded files. Each download uses the password entered for that download.

Only the owning vendor can list, create, edit, or download estimates for a lead. All management routes require authentication; downloads are rate limited. The existing buyer-verified Private Estimate workflow remains separate, with its controlled verification and scope confirmation transitions.

Phase 2 supports manual PDF downloads. Phase 3 adds an explicit email action with a protected attachment, unique scheduling link, and delivery history; see VENDOR_ESTIMATE_DELIVERY.md. Password release remains manual.

Validation: `vendor/bin/phpunit tests/Feature/VendorLeadEstimateTest.php tests/Feature/VendorBuyerLeadTest.php`.
