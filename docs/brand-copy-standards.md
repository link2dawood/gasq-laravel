# GASQ Brand & Copy Standards

Single source of truth for how we name things and write copy across the site,
emails, reports, and marketing. Developers and content writers: follow this so
terminology stays consistent everywhere.

Last updated: 2026 · Owner: GASQ

---

## 1. Brand architecture (the hierarchy)

Use these terms for these purposes — do not blur the levels.

| Level | Name | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Company / brand | **GetASecurityQuoteNow** / **GASQ** | The company. Full name on first mention; "GASQ" thereafter. |
| Category | **Security Procurement Intelligence** | What kind of product GASQ is. |
| Platform positioning | **The Financial Operating System for Security Procurement™** | Supporting positioning line — never the *sole* explanation of the product. |
| Primary methodology | **Cost to Protect™** | GASQ's independent cost calculation. |
| Procurement process | **Validate Before You Estimate™** | The order of operations we teach buyers. |
| Workforce / pricing analysis | **Workforce-to-Post™** | The staffing/bill-rate breakdown. |
| Certification | **GASQ Certified™** | Reports/analyses that passed GASQ's review. |
| Primary buyer promise | **Know What Security Should Cost Before You Buy.** | The homepage headline promise. |

**Lead with the promise, support with the positioning.** Homepages and buyer
pages lead with *"Know What Security Should Cost Before You Buy."* The *"Financial
Operating System…"* line is supporting context, not the headline.

## 2. Exact spelling & capitalization

Write these **exactly** as shown:

- **GASQ** (all caps, never "Gasq" or "gasq")
- **GetASecurityQuoteNow** (one word, camelCase; the domain is getasecurityquotenow.com)
- **Cost to Protect™** (title case; "to" lowercase)
- **Cost to Deliver™** (the vendor-side counterpart)
- **Workforce-to-Post™** (hyphenated)
- **GASQ Certified™**
- **Validate Before You Estimate™**
- **Know Before You Buy** (buyers) · **Know Before You Bid** (vendors) — pick the one that matches the audience
- **The Financial Operating System for Security Procurement™**

Do **not** write: "cost to protect" (lowercase as a product name), "Cost To
Protect" (over-capitalized "To"), "Workforce to Post" (missing hyphens),
"GASQ certified" (lowercase c).

## 3. Trademark (™ / ®) usage

**Mark the first prominent use per page, then plain text after.** Do not put ™
on every mention — it clutters body copy.

- ✅ *"GASQ calculates the **Cost to Protect™** for every job. The Cost to Protect
  is independent of any vendor quote…"* (marked once, plain after)
- ❌ *"The Cost to Protect™ report shows the Cost to Protect™, and your Cost to
  Protect™ savings…"* (over-marked)

In code, use the HTML entity **`&trade;`** (and `&reg;`) rather than the literal
`™`/`®` characters, for consistency and encoding safety.

Genuine common-noun usage stays lowercase and unmarked: *"the cost to deliver the
service"* is a sentence, not the product name **Cost to Deliver™**.

## 4. Role & document vocabulary

Use one word per concept. Don't swap synonyms mid-flow.

| Term | Means | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Buyer** | The organization procuring security services | Not "client" or "customer" in product copy. |
| **Vendor** | The licensed security provider that bids and delivers | Not "supplier" or "contractor" in product copy. |
| **Estimate** | GASQ's independent, calculated cost — what the service *should* cost | Produced by the Instant Estimator / calculators. Never a vendor's price. |
| **Quote** | A price a **vendor** proposes | GASQ *validates* a quote against the estimate. |
| **Appraisal** | GASQ's formal, GASQ Certified™ Cost to Protect™ report for a job | The document, not the number. |
| **Offer** | The buyer's posted job/opportunity extended to vendors | The "security job offer announcement." |
| **Bid** | A vendor's formal response (price + terms) to an offer | Vendors submit bids; buyers review them. |

**Key distinction:** an **estimate** is GASQ's number; a **quote/bid** is a
vendor's number. Never call a vendor's price an "estimate," and never call GASQ's
calculation a "quote."

## 5. Positioning principles (say these; mean these)

- **GASQ is not another quote-comparison website.** Before vendors say what they
  want to charge, GASQ helps determine what the service *should realistically cost
  to deliver.*
- **Price validation before price comparison.** We validate the real cost first,
  then compare vendor pricing against it — not the other way around.
- **Scope First. Validation Second. Price Last.**
- **Validate Before You Estimate™.**
- GASQ never treats a vendor's quote as the starting point for what protection
  should cost.

Position GASQ primarily as **security procurement / pricing intelligence** — not
as a lead marketplace or a quote aggregator.
